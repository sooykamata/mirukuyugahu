Yanbaru — The Blue Zone of Okinawa

Nestled in the World Natural Heritage forests of northern Okinawa, Yanbaru is one of the world's rare Blue Zones — a place where people live long, connected to nature, food, and community.

At MIRUKUYUGAHU, we invite you to slow down and experience this way of life firsthand. Cook over a wood-fired stove. Rest on ryukyu tatami. Share the rhythms of daily life with your host, just next door.

This is not a hotel. This is a life.
